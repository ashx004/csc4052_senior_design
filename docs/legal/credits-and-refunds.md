# AI Credits, Refunds and End of Service

_Effective October 18, 2026. Last updated October 19, 2026._

Catalyst is run by ZonoCSC as a not-for-profit service. AI features
cost us money every time they run, so we pass that cost to you **at cost**:
what the AI provider charges, plus the payment fees described in section 2,
and nothing else. We do not add a markup.

## 1. How AI credits work

- **Credits are dollars.** Your balance is shown in US dollars. Each time you use an
  AI feature (chat, quizzes, flashcards, OCR, advising and similar), we deduct what
  the AI provider charged us for that request, based on the tokens it reports,
  plus the provider's fee described in section 2.
- **Prices follow the provider.** We charge the provider's current published rate
  at the time of the request. If a provider changes its prices, the same balance
  buys more or fewer AI requests. Credits are not a fixed number of tokens.
- **Estimates.** Your AI credit page shows what common tasks typically cost, and
  your balance is always visible in the menu. Estimates are approximate. You are
  charged the actual cost, which may be higher or lower.
- **Stopping a reply.** If you stop a reply partway, you pay for the part the AI
  had already written, since the provider charges us for it.
- **Reserved, then settled.** While a request runs we temporarily hold a small
  amount of your balance. When it finishes we charge the actual cost and release
  the rest.
- **When your balance runs out,** AI features pause until you add credits. Everything
  else keeps working: notes, calendar, saved study sets and exports.
- **Free trial credit.** New accounts with a verified email address get a small
  free credit (currently $0.25) to try the AI features. It is limited to one per
  person and email address, may end at any time, and cannot be refunded or
  exchanged for cash.
- **Minimum and maximum.** Top-ups start at $5. To limit how much money
  sits unused near the end of service, a balance cannot go above $20.

## 2. Fees we pass on

We can only run at cost if we don't absorb anyone's fees, so two are passed on
exactly, with nothing added:

- **Card processing.** Card payments go through Stripe, which charges a
  processing fee (currently 2.9% + $0.30 per payment in the US). It comes out of
  your top-up and is shown before you pay. For example, a $10.00 payment adds
  about $9.41 of credits. Larger top-ups lose a smaller share to the fixed part
  of the fee.
- **AI provider credit fee.** We buy AI from our provider, OpenRouter, with
  prepaid credit, and OpenRouter keeps a fee on each purchase (currently 5.5%).
  We add the same share to each request: a request that costs us $0.00100 in AI
  usage is charged to you as about $0.00106. If the fee changes, the charge
  follows it.

## 3. Bring your own key (BYOK)

If you connect your own AI provider API key, requests made with it are billed by
that provider directly to you, under that provider's terms. Catalyst does not
charge credits for those requests. You are responsible for that key's spending
limits and for keeping it secret. You can remove it at any time. We store keys
encrypted and use them only to make the requests you initiate.

## 4. Refunds

You buy credits to pay for the AI tokens that generate your responses.

- **Used credits are not refundable.** Once you run an AI request, we have
  already paid the provider for it.
- **Unused balance is refundable on request.** Ask from Settings or at
  contact@zonocsc.com. We refund to your original payment method and deduct only
  Stripe's processing fee, because Stripe does not return it to us. Refunds are
  made from your most recent purchases first. If a card can no longer be
  refunded, we will contact you to arrange another method.
- **Deleting your account** refunds your unused balance first, the same way.
- **Our errors.** If an AI request fails because of an error on our side, we do not
  charge for it. If you were charged anyway, contact us and we will credit your
  balance.
- **Billing mistakes.** If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, we
  refund the difference in full, including the processing fee. Please contact us
  before opening a card dispute. It is faster and we will fix it.
- **Fraud.** Balances bought with stolen payment details, or reversed through a
  card dispute, are removed and are not refundable.

## 5. End of service

Catalyst is currently planned to run until **March 18, 2027**. We may extend
it. If we do, we will post the new date.

When the service is going to end:

1. **At least 30 days' notice** by email and in the app.
2. **New credit purchases stop** on the day of the notice. AI features keep working
   with your existing balance until the end date.
3. **Data export.** You can download your data (notes, study sets, chat history,
   documents) from Settings until the end date.
4. **Automatic refunds of unused balance.** You do not need to ask. Within 30
   days after the end date, we refund every remaining balance to
   its original payment method, minus Stripe's processing fee, which Stripe does
   not return to us. If a card refund is not possible, we will contact you to
   arrange another method.
5. **Data deletion.** Within 60 days after the end date, we delete account
   data, stored documents and AI vectors, except records we must keep by law (for
   example payment records).

If we have to stop suddenly for reasons outside our control, steps 4 and 5
still apply.
